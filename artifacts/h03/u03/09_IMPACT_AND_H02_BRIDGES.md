# Impact and H02 bridges

Impact preview is read-only and lists future allocations affected by Staff/date/service scope. Ordinary Business Hours, Staff schedule, eligibility removal, and offboarding commands acquire the same Business guard before aggregate locks and reject incompatible commitments.

Urgent suspension/revocation remains effective immediately and records OPEN commitment conflicts; it never silently cancels allocations. Integration tests prove schedule and service removal rejection plus urgent suspension/conflict persistence. Commercial terms/publication changes remain prospective as specified.
